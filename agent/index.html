      About this demo

      A concierge that books the job, not just answers questions

      • What this is. The website of a fictional Denver HVAC and roofing company, with an AI concierge (Riley) in the corner. Everything on the page is sample data; nothing you type goes anywhere.
      • What to try. Say your AC died. Say another company is cheaper. Say you smell gas. Say you're renting, or in Colorado Springs. Then open What the business sees.
      • How it works. One system prompt (triage, one question at a time, objection playbooks, safety guardrails) and four tools: note the lead, capture the lead, book an arrival window, hand off to a human. This page runs a scripted engine so it needs no API key; the same prompt and tools run on Claude in the repo.
